Malvani Kitchen

Tastes of the Konkan

Every dish tells a story of the coast — coconut, kokum, fresh catch and age-old spice blends passed down through generations of Konkan households.

Seafood

Malvani Prawns Curry

Plump tiger prawns slow-cooked in a fiery coconut-based Malvani masala, best savoured with steaming rice or bhakri.

Beverage

Sol Kadhi

The Konkan coast's signature pink drink — kokum extract blended with fresh coconut milk. Cooling, tangy, unforgettable.

Breakfast

Ghavan

Thin, lace-like rice crepes made from fermented rice batter — a light and delicate Konkani breakfast staple.

Main Course

Kombdi Vade

The crown jewel of Malvani cuisine — spiced chicken curry served alongside crispy, fluffy deep-fried rice-flour vade.

Bread

Tandalachi Bhakari

Hand-patted rice flatbread, smoky from the griddle and soft within — the everyday bread of every Konkan home.

Seafood

Kaji Masala

A bold dry preparation of crab in whole Malvani spices — fragrant, spicy and deeply satisfying with a coastal warmth.

Vegetarian

Phanasachi Bhaji

Raw jackfruit stir-fried with fresh coconut, mustard seeds and Konkani spices — a beloved monsoon-season vegetable dish.

Breakfast

Shirvale

Steamed rice noodles served with coconut milk and jaggery — a gentle, nourishing way to begin a Konkan morning.

Dessert

Ukdiche Modak

Steamed rice-flour dumplings filled with sweet coconut and jaggery — a sacred and indulgent Konkan sweet treat.

Signature

Malvani Thali

Our complete tasting experience — a curated spread of fish curry, rice, bhakri, solkadhi, bhaji and seasonal sides on a single plate.

Beyond the Resort

Explore the Konkan

Step outside and discover a coastline rich with ancient forts, hidden beaches, rock formations and marine wonders — all within easy reach.

Beach ~12 km

Kondura Beach

A crescent of golden sand framed by coconut groves, largely untouched and perfect for early-morning solitude.

Heritage ~35 km

Sindhudurg Fort

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's magnificent sea fort built on a rocky island — a testament to Maratha naval power.

Beach ~38 km

Tarkarli

Crystal-clear waters ideal for snorkelling and scuba diving, where the Karli river meets the sea in spectacular fashion.

Nature ~8 km

Nivti Rocks

Dramatic volcanic rock formations jutting into the sea, lit gold at sunset — a photographer's favourite vantage point.

Heritage ~18 km

Yashwantgad Fort

A lesser-visited coastal fort perched above the sea, offering sweeping views of the Konkan shoreline and surrounding forests.

Beach ~22 km

Vengurla Beach

A wide, tranquil beach at the southern tip of Maharashtra with shallow waters and magnificent views of nearby islands.

Nature ~28 km

Turtle Point

A protected nesting ground for Olive Ridley sea turtles — witness the remarkable spectacle of hatchlings heading to the sea.

Heritage ~24 km

Vengurla Lighthouse

One of the oldest lighthouses on the Konkan coast, standing guard since the colonial era with views stretching to the horizon.

Beach ~6 km

Sagreshwar Beach

The closest beach to the resort — quiet, clean and ideal for an evening stroll as fishing boats return with their catch.

Beach ~14 km

Shiroda Velaghar

A long, secluded stretch of sand beloved by locals, where the pace of life slows to the rhythm of the tides.

Nature ~20 km

Khavne Village

An untouched Konkan village surrounded by paddy fields and cashew orchards — a living window into coastal rural life.

Beach ~10 km

Waigani Beach

Hidden behind a canopy of casuarina trees, Waigani rewards those who seek it with pristine sands and complete seclusion.

Heritage ~25 km

Vengurla Bandar

The old Portuguese trading port, with weathered warehouses and a bustling fish market that comes alive at dawn.

Beach ~40 km

Tarkarli Backwaters

Glide through serene backwaters by kayak or traditional dugout — an entirely different perspective on the Konkan landscape.
