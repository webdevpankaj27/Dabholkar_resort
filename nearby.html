Ratnagiri · Sindhudurg · Konkan

Explore the Konkan

Ancient forts, hidden beaches, volcanic rock formations and marine sanctuaries — all within a short drive of Dabholkar Resort.

Beach ~12 km

Kondura Beach

A crescent of golden sand framed by coconut groves — largely untouched and perfect for early-morning solitude.

Heritage ~35 km

Sindhudurg Fort

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's magnificent sea fort built on a rocky island — a testament to Maratha naval power.

Beach ~38 km

Tarkarli

Crystal-clear waters ideal for snorkelling and scuba diving, where the Karli river meets the sea in spectacular fashion.

Nature ~8 km

Nivti Rocks

Dramatic volcanic rock formations jutting into the sea, lit gold at sunset — a photographer's favourite vantage point.

Heritage ~18 km

Yashwantgad Fort

A lesser-visited coastal fort perched above the sea, offering sweeping views of the Konkan shoreline and surrounding forests.

Beach ~22 km

Vengurla Beach

A wide, tranquil beach with shallow waters and magnificent views of nearby islands, beloved by locals at dusk.

Nature ~28 km

Turtle Point

A protected nesting ground for Olive Ridley sea turtles — witness the remarkable spectacle of hatchlings heading to sea.

Heritage ~24 km

Vengurla Lighthouse

One of the oldest lighthouses on the Konkan coast, standing guard since the colonial era with views stretching to the horizon.

Beach ~6 km

Sagreshwar Beach

The closest beach to the resort — quiet, clean, and ideal for an evening stroll as fishing boats return with their catch.

Beach ~14 km

Shiroda Velaghar

A long, secluded stretch of sand beloved by locals, where the pace of life slows to the rhythm of the tides.

Nature ~20 km

Khavne Village

An untouched Konkan village surrounded by paddy fields and cashew orchards — a living window into coastal rural life.

Beach ~10 km

Waigani Beach

Hidden behind a canopy of casuarina trees, Waigani rewards those who seek it with pristine sands and complete seclusion.

Heritage ~25 km

Vengurla Bandar

The old Portuguese trading port, with weathered warehouses and a bustling fish market that comes alive at dawn.

Beach ~40 km

Tarkarli Backwaters

Glide through serene backwaters by kayak or traditional dugout — an entirely different perspective on the Konkan landscape.
